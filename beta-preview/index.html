Quick help

How to upload a CSV file

  1. Go to where your roster or bank statement lives — your school's system, your bank, or Venmo.
  2. Look for a button like this one. It's usually labeled “Export to CSV” or something similar:
  3. Click it. This downloads a CSV file to your computer, usually into your Downloads folder.
  4. Come back here, click the upload button, find that CSV file, and choose it.

That's it — VendorFlow takes it from there.
